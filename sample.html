Sample: Legal Reasoning

Principle: A person who voluntarily causes a foreseeable loss by failing to take reasonable care may be liable for that loss, subject to the facts and applicable law.

Fact: A shopkeeper leaves a known spill on a busy floor without warning customers. A customer slips and suffers loss.

Question: Which reasoning approach is strongest?

  1. Apply the principle to the relevant facts.
  2. Ignore the facts and rely only on the outcome.
  3. Assume liability solely because an injury occurred.
  4. Choose an unrelated legal rule.

Answer: A — The task is to apply the stated principle to the facts.
